### 🔍 Detailed Project Case Studies

#### 1. SaaS Customer Retention & Churn Analysis
* **Business Context:** In subscription-based products, minor increases in early-stage churn compound into substantial long-term recurring revenue loss[cite: 1].
* **Data & Methodology:** Structured user activity logs into monthly acquisition cohorts to measure retention decay across lifecycle periods and isolate behavioral patterns associated with churn risk[cite: 1].
* **Dashboard Capabilities:** Interactive cohort retention tables, churn trend visualizations, and filtered views of at-risk users to support proactive customer success outreach[cite: 1].
* **Analytical Impact:** Shifts retention management from reactive cancellation reporting to early behavioral intervention[cite: 1].

#### 2. Customer Segmentation (RFM Modeling)
* **Business Context:** Uniform marketing campaigns waste budget on disengaged users while under-investing in high-lifetime-value customers.
* **Data & Methodology:** Engineered Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) metrics at the customer level, applying quantile-based scoring to group users into distinct behavioral segments[cite: 1].
* **Dashboard Capabilities:** Dynamic segment distribution breakdowns, revenue contribution comparisons across tiers, and tailored retention and upsell strategies per customer group[cite: 1].
* **Analytical Impact:** Enables precision targeting for VIP loyalty rewards, cross-sell promotions, and automated churn-prevention workflows.

#### 3. Retail Sales & Profitability Analytics
* **Business Context:** Strong top-line sales volume frequently disguises low-margin SKUs, heavy discounting, or underperforming geographic territories[cite: 1].
* **Data & Methodology:** Evaluated revenue alongside cost and margin structures across product categories and regions to separate high-volume revenue drivers from true bottom-line profit generators[cite: 1].
* **Dashboard Capabilities:** Cross-filtered profit vs. revenue comparisons, regional performance scorecards, and category-level margin variance views[cite: 1].
* **Analytical Impact:** Highlights high-revenue, low-margin products requiring pricing review and surfaces profitable regional markets primed for expansion[cite: 1].

#### 4. Sales & Inventory Risk Monitoring
* **Business Context:** Balancing working capital tied up in excess inventory against the direct revenue loss of stockouts requires continuous visibility into demand velocity[cite: 1].
* **Data & Methodology:** Integrated historical monthly sales trends with current inventory balances to evaluate stock coverage and flag items approaching critical reorder thresholds[cite: 1].
* **Dashboard Capabilities:** Time-series sales tracking, stock-level distribution charts, and automated low-stock risk prioritization tables[cite: 1].
* **Analytical Impact:** Provides operations teams with an early-warning system to prioritize replenishment before stockouts occur[cite: 1].

#### 5. Marketing Campaign ROI & CAC Attribution
* **Business Context:** Growth teams must continuously reallocate budget toward channels that acquire customers efficiently rather than chasing raw click or lead volume[cite: 1].
* **Data & Methodology:** Standardized campaign performance across acquisition channels to evaluate spend efficiency through Conversion Rate, Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), and Return on Investment (ROI)[cite: 1].
* **Dashboard Capabilities:** Side-by-side channel efficiency comparisons, spend-to-revenue breakdowns, and ROI ranking tables[cite: 1].
* **Analytical Impact:** Supports data-backed budget reallocation by exposing underperforming campaigns with inflated CAC alongside scalable, high-ROI channels[cite: 1].
